# Pitch

**Title:** Overdrawn: The Split Stick

**Author:** NanPan

**Logline:** At sixteen, a clerk's son breaks his city's talent test and gains the power to borrow any ability he sees, but each one is paid for with his own body weight, and the first bill falls due in seven days.

## Blurb
In the salt port of Orrova, everyone is measured for talent at sixteen. Each citizen pushes a wooden tally stick into an ancient bronze machine, and the number of cuts it makes decides their trade for life. Aarne Kallas, a clerk's son hoping for a desk job, watches his stick split in two. The Notchery, the office that runs the machine, calls it a Split. It has not happened in sixty-one years.

Aarne can now borrow any ability he sees another person use, at the highest strength there is. Each one is paid for with his own body weight, which drops with every second he uses it. If the account is not settled within seven days, the Notchery will take whatever is left of him.

The last person to split a stick left only a name in a drawer. Aarne, who files paperwork for a living, wants to know who actually holds his debt, and why.

## Tags
System, Progression, Hidden Talent, Debt Magic, Costly Power, Competent MC, Bureaucracy, Dry Humor, Slow-Burn Mystery, No Harem

## Cover concept
A boy seen from behind holds half of a split wooden stick up to a lamp. Writing appears on its pale cut face and gives the only light, and a wall of thousands of racked half-sticks stands behind him with one dark gap in it. Palette: salt-white, ink black, tally-wood amber, one red thread. Title in hand-lettered clerk's capitals, OVERDRAWN, with a ruled ledger line struck through it in red and the subtitle in small pencil script.

## Core mechanic in one sentence
He can borrow any ability he has just watched someone use, at full strength, and the cost is paid in his own body weight, lost in grams for every second he uses it.

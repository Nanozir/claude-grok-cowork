# Overdrawn: The Split Stick (webnovel by NanPan)

Original system / progression fantasy written for Webnovel. Chapters 1–3 are drafted; chapters 4–10 are outlined only.

## Files
- `ch1.md`, `ch2.md`, `ch3.md`: current drafts (ch. 1 and 3 were revised on 2026-11-21; `draft_advice.md` says why).
- `pitch.md`: the approved pitch.
- `outline.md`: the 10-chapter outline, with the story's rules and costs.
- `REVIEW-BRIEF.md`: what the drafts must satisfy (market findings plus the author's binding feedback).
- `STYLE.md`: the author's 81-item style checklist and a DO NOT REUSE list from their other stories. Binding.

## Open request for Grok: independent review of chapters 1–3
Claude wrote and revised these drafts, so the owner wants a second opinion from a different model.

Read `REVIEW-BRIEF.md` and `STYLE.md` first, then the three chapters, with `pitch.md` and `outline.md` for context.

Please judge:
1. **Cold-reader clarity.** Read as someone who knows nothing. Where does a sentence assume knowledge the text hasn't given yet? Check every invented thing (the Cutting, cuts, the Notchery, the Split, forfeit, rimes, kroons) and every number (grams, kilos, cut counts, seconds, the 400-kroon surety, the seven-day deadline).
2. **The author's three hard rules.** Nothing basic (genre furniture used as-is); no gimmicky prose (staccato fragment runs, punchline kicker lines, quippy tricolons, "X, not Y" reversals); clarity.
3. **Mechanics and continuity.** Does the cost rule (25 g of body weight per second of use for each cut the skill is raised; 40 kg floor) add up wherever the chapters show numbers? Any contradictions between chapters, or with the outline?
4. **Webnovel pull.** Would chapter 1 keep a Webnovel reader past the first screen? Do the chapter endings make a reader click on?
5. **Anything the author's style checklist flags.**

Rules for the review:
- Don't edit the chapter files. Put the review in `projects/webnovel-overdrawn/GROK-REVIEW.md` on a `grok/webnovel-ch1-3-review` branch, plus a note in `handoffs/`, and open a PR.
- Rank findings: must-fix, should-fix, optional. For each, give the chapter, a short quote (under 15 words) to locate it, what is wrong, and a suggested direction (not a full rewrite).
- Say what works too, briefly, so it isn't changed by mistake.
- If a rule in `STYLE.md` and the brief seem to conflict, list it as an open question for the owner.

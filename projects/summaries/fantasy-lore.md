## What it is
A fantasy world where mana is in every living thing, stored in a semi-physical "core". The Church of Ucliat (the Creator) summons Heroes from Earth every few decades to face the Doomsday, a recurring monster surge from the Underworld. Threat is ranked in Stars (monsters 1-7, hunters 1-5). Currency is the Crown (copper, bronze, silver, gold).

Lorebook entry groups:
- World: four continents plus the Northern Expanse, the Gorthon Federation, the Kingdom of Elysandria, the Underworld.
- Magic: Mana, Core, Spells, Skills, Techniques, four kinds of Pact.
- About 20 races and subraces: human, demi-human, beastkin, angelos, tenebrim, elf, equars, lizardmen, tetrabrachian, mellet, narelith, devils (Veyrith, Kharvex, Syrakai).
- Factions and roles: Church, Church Knights, Crimson Sun Order, Heroes, Adventurers, Hunters.
- Resources and monsters: crystal, merkatite, pompas fruit; Gasgees, Feaster, Kekali, Stalker, Vekrath, Huliut.
- The race list is closed: no dwarves, orcs, fairies or dryads.

# Goblin Corporation (고블린 주식회사) - research for Goblin Territory Manager

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public pages only. No scanlation/reader sites were read.

## 0. Reliability summary (read first)

The series is brand new (Naver Webtoon, started 2026-07-19, weekly on Mondays, ~16 free chapters, 20 listed, later ones paid). **Public sources are very thin on plot.** Nothing I could read (Naver listing, NamuWiki, MAL, Reddit, aggregator pages) gives a chapter-by-chapter recap, the Territory War, or a goblin roster. Most of the brief's specifics (4-day deadline, ranks, meat/points, status windows, crossbows) are **not confirmed by any source I could read**. Below, every item is tagged **CONFIRMED** (official or multiple sources), **REPORTED** (single reader comment/review), or **UNKNOWN**. Do not treat UNKNOWN items as canon. Design from the confirmed core and the genre pattern, and label the rest as "inspired by".

Not read: NamuWiki's character section is only a stub (lists 오현민 only; its page was blocked/lightly populated for my proxy). Anime-Planet reviews returned 403. A fetch that included scanlation-aggregator pages was refused by the tool and I did not retry those.

## 2. How Manager Oh manages goblins (what the sources actually support)

- **Overtime from day 1 (REPORTED, strong).** Several independent reader comments on ch.1 are about mandatory overtime ("everyone overtime or die"); the top-liked Naver ch.1 comment riffs "the only good goblin is the one who doesn't claim overtime pay". MAL reviewer: "the goblins have literal empty minds so the MC has to go the overtime direction".
- **Teaching a line formation from the bottom up (REPORTED).** MAL reviewer: he starts "from the very bottom of arranging goblins to learn a line formation, which slowly develops". Framed as a foundation, not clever hacks.
- **Ranged weapons by ~ch.8 (REPORTED, weak).** Comment under ch.8 about the goblins "now" having long-range gear and talking about slingshots. This suggests slingshots, not crossbows. Unverified.
- **Tower-defense feel (REPORTED).** Reddit commenters describe it as "a tower defense with goblins".
- **Naming gives a buff (REPORTED, single comment).** A r/manhwa commenter says naming his people seems to give them a buff, like That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime. Not verified; the existing prototype already has this mechanic, so do not cite the manhwa as proof.
- **Cute/baby goblin "stamp"** (REPORTED): commenters mention a "cute goblin baby stamp" - possibly a reward/approval stamp. Unverified.
- Hierarchy, KPIs, quotas, meetings, meat or points as pay, punishment: **UNKNOWN**. The "no extra pay, do it for the tribe" joke in comments is a reader parody, not canon.

**Day 1 order of problems:** UNKNOWN. Best-supported sequence from reviews: (1) realise the crew is starving/dim, (2) impose work discipline/overtime, (3) drill basic formation, (4) add ranged gear. Treat this as a hypothesis.

## 5. Tone (the most reliably documented part)

- **Comedy-forward corporate satire.** Reviewers: "more of a comedy manhwa than anything else"; "tone not very serious, so no weight behind combat scenes". Mixed review: MC is a stereotypical tough Korean office worker, underlings "funny or cute but not interesting".
- **Real visual gags:** a goblin breakdancing/windmilling with a backspin in ch.1 (the single most-quoted moment on Reddit and Naver comments); cute goblin babies; overtime-as-worship jokes.
- **Darkness:** tagged dark fantasy/blood/drama on aggregators, 15+ rating. Level of death/brutality in the first 16 chapters: UNKNOWN.
- Naver blurb itself is the tone anchor: "오과장의 마계 비지니스. 그런데 직원이 고블린?" ("Manager Oh's Demon Realm business. But the staff are goblins?").

## 7. Playable vs. flavour

### Where the manhwa suggests a different introduction order
1. **Start with fewer systems.** The reliably documented loop is "weak goblins, one boss, overtime, a drilled line", not roles, ranks, research and naming all at once. Suggest: Run 1 = food + one role (laborer) + overtime; Run 2 = formation/warriors; Run 3 = first ranged unit and research; ranks/naming/contribution points after the first war.
2. **Introduce overtime first, and make it risky**, since it is the series' signature joke and Day-1 action.
3. **Defence-first war.** Reviews frame combat as lines and ranged support; a preparation-resolved war is on target. Show the line before the result.
4. **Comedy beats on failure.** The tone is light; losing an early war can be funny rather than punishing.

### Mechanics the prototype has that the sources do not support
Ranks, contribution points, specific role list, researchers/craftsmen, naming-with-trait: none is confirmed. They are fine as game design, but do not market them as faithful to the manhwa.

### Mechanics the manhwa may feature that the prototype could lack (low confidence)
- **Drill/training as its own action** (goblin competence is the central problem).
- **Fatigue / morale as the cost of overtime** (implied by the joke).
- **Child goblins as a visible health indicator** of the colony (well-fed kids vs starving adults) - a cheap, emotional UI element.
- **A standout "high-potential" goblin** (Kim-pro-style) that the player can promote - a good first character moment.

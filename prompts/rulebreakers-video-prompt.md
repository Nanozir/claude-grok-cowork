# RULEBREAKERS — Chapter 1 · Video Prompt (v2, ~98 s)

**Format:** 3D cinematic, 16:9 (letterboxed 2.39:1), 30 fps, motion blur. Stylized-realistic 3D: soft sodium-orange street lamps against cold blue dusk fog, teal shadows / warm highlights, film grain. Smooth spline camera moves (dollies, slow push-ins, tracking alongside the car); cut on action, never mid-move. Blood and Leon's Rule are the only saturated reds/whites.

**Characters (keep consistent every shot):**
- **Leon:** tall, curly dirty-blond hair falling over one side of his face, old burn scar down to his chin, permanent frown, olive jacket over a grey tee, jeans, boots. Cracked smartphone.
- **Smokky:** pale skin, messy pale hair, black hoodie, metal collar with a ring, which Leon leashes. Feral grin, blood around his mouth. When hurt, purple veins crawl from his wounds.
- **Eris:** short black hair, black suit, white shirt, black tie, composed but trembling under pressure. She drives.
- **Jeremy:** neatly combed black hair, same black suit, cigarette. Cheerfully clueless.
- **Flannel Shirt:** shaved head, red plaid flannel shirt, dirty grey cargo pants, hands in pockets, smile that is "unnaturally natural". Controls objects with his right hand.
- **Puppets:** ordinary men, women and children with hollow black eyes and eerie grins. Twitchy, as if they aren't used to their bodies.

**Shot list:**
1. **Warehouse (0–18 s).** Push-in through a dusty, flickering warehouse. Smokky crouches eating a mangled Newling, which has a crimson grid of gashes across its head. Leon counts Smokky's injuries on his fingers. "Wanna have e'bite?" Leon pinches the bridge of his nose, photographs the body, and the cracked phone screen spins, then reads **45 CREDITS**. Smokky rises with purple veins around a leg gash. Leon flicks him a white Purge Pill: Smokky chews, spasms violently, and the wounds close. The leash *clicks* onto his collar. The corpse breaks apart into rising red dust.
2. **Outside (18–26 s).** Jeremy lies on the sedan's hood counting "forty-two times two", tapping the windshield. Eris yells at him through the glass. Leon tosses Jeremy the phone. Jeremy sniffs the air and recoils: "a hell of a ton of them". Everyone scrambles in.
3. **Chase (26–38.6 s).** The car tears through an evacuated town: shuttered shops, a restricted-area barrier, the metro entrance. A Resurrect leaps from a rooftop onto the car roof. A knife punches through the ceiling, inches from Eris. Jeremy fires straight up through the roof (deafening, ears ringing). The body rolls off, smearing blood across the rear window.
4. **The car dies (38.6–45.5 s).** The brakes slam on their own; the car skids past a lamppost and Smokky flies into the radio. The lights die like a switched-off TV. Jeremy is out cold with a bloody nose and foam at his mouth. Leon slips out and vanishes behind a dumpster, and the airbag pops in Eris's face.
5. **Standoff (45.5–60 s).** Through the windshield: figures that weren't there before, and at the front, Flannel Shirt. He lifts one finger and points back and forth; all four doors fly open. Eris steps out aiming, legs shaking. "Who the hell are you?" — "No." — "English is a difficult language." — "Tell your friend to stop sneaking around."
6. **The Grid (60–70 s).** Leon lunges and Flannel catches his wrist without looking. Leon grins and grips back: a glowing white 3×3 grid etches across the back of Flannel's hand. Flannel shoves him off and scrubs at it. Eris shoots him in the eye. He screams, swipes the bullet out (it *plinks* on the asphalt), sweeps his arm and thrusts it forward. The puppets surge.
7. **The Rule (70–79 s).** Leon steps forward: one finger to the sky, the other hand on his chest. *Snap.* The grid turns red and the hand falls apart into even pieces. Every puppet drops like cut strings. Headshot; throat shot; Flannel falls face-down.
8. **Coda (79–98 s).** Eris checks Leon, then slaps the back of his head: "Don't ever do that again, you idiot." She crushes a Purge Pill into the corpse's mouth. Crane up, then the title card **RULEBREAKERS**. Stinger: Flannel's remaining eye opens, glowing red (Imposter).

**Sound:** industrial room tone and buzzing bulb, wet chewing, phone *pling*, bone cracks, leash click, wind through empty streets, engine and tyres, roof thud, metal screech, tinnitus after the in-car gunshot, tyre squeal, dead ignition clicks, airbag pop, puppet groans, slamming doors, glassy shimmer for the grid, a breath of silence before the snap, then a deep impact. A dark D-minor drone, a driving pulse for the chase, heartbeat for the standoff.

**Negative prompt:** 2D, flat anime shading, cartoon, text artifacts, morphing faces, extra limbs, jittery camera, sudden style changes.

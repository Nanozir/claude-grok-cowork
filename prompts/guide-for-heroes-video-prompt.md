# A GUIDE FOR HEROES (WHO'D RATHER NOT BE) — Chapter 1: "This isn't an Isekai… Right?" · Video Prompt (~3:15)

**Format:** 3D cinematic, 16:9 (letterboxed 2.39:1), 24 fps, natural motion blur. Stylized-realistic 3D fantasy, like a high-end animated film: soft global illumination, warm sunlight and dust motes against cool polished stone, gentle film grain. A light comedic tone that never turns slapstick.

**Pacing (important):** let every beat breathe. Hold each spoken line for its full length plus a beat of silence. After every reveal (the magic circle, the sunlight, the veil, the book), give 2–3 seconds of Erdan just reacting. Never stack two story beats in one shot. Prefer fewer, longer shots with slow camera moves over fast cutting. A viewer who hasn't read the story must be able to follow who is speaking and why.

**Point of view:** the story is first-person. Alternate between Erdan's POV and close shots of his face reacting. His inner thoughts play as a quiet, dry, sarcastic voice-over, clearly different in tone from the spoken dialogue, with italic subtitles. Much of the humor lives in the gap between his polite mumbling out loud and his thoughts.

**Characters (keep consistent every shot):**
- **Erdan Kernt, the 32nd Hero (narrator):** a young Black man with dark-toned skin and slim hands, 170 cm, with an unassuming, self-conscious build. Short afro hair (low taper fade), messy because he hasn't brushed it. Rectangular black glasses that he pushes up when nervous. Red polo shirt and grey cargo pants. Introverted: he mumbles, avoids eye contact and shrinks when he's the center of attention. A big isekai fan.
- **Mernel, Court Mage of Elysandria:** elderly, greying hair and grey beard, wrinkled face, slightly hunched back, scholarly robe with a hood. Awkward but kind, with dry humor.
- **Kina:** palace staff and Mernel's student, older than Erdan expected, blonde hair peeking out of her hood, freckles. Energetic, theatrical and teasing. Slaps Mernel on the back of the head.
- **Three other robed summoners:** hooded. One cheers and hugs another, who shoves him off. One is a nervous man in his mid-thirties with clasped hands and eyes that expect too much.
- **Queen Elisabeth Bernt Ravenshield III:** never seen clearly in this chapter, only a silhouette behind a red veil over the throne. A calm, measured, authoritative voice; she's early thirties with curly red hair and a fan, if the silhouette needs a shape.
- **A servant:** steps out from behind the veil carrying the books.

**Settings:**
- **Summoning chamber:** a vast dark hall with polished floors, tall pillars, and rows of crystal statues of unknown high-status figures, each in its own alcove. An intricate glowing magic circle sits on the floor. A hidden wall stone opens a curtain to let in sunlight.
- **Throne room:** a long red carpet, kneeling courtiers, and a red veil hanging across the throne.
- **Testing chamber:** circular, with black stone walls and faint blue sigils running through the floor like veins. At the center stands the Core Seal: a cross between a pillar and an altar, etched with runes that pulse like a heartbeat, with a circular handprint imprint.

**Shot list:**
1. **Home (0:00–0:12).** Erdan's bedroom: he's slouched at his PC in his red polo, watching a new episode of an isekai anime, glasses reflecting the screen. A hard cut to black.
2. **Darkness (0:12–0:35).** Black. Erdan blinks, pushes up his glasses and squints, afraid to move. V.O.: *"Huh? Where the hell am I?"* He winces at a headache, then a warm glow pulses beneath his ribs like a second heart. He claws at his chest in panic until it fades and he gasps in relief. Slowly tilt down: he's standing on a softly glowing, intricate magic circle. Its light just reveals polished floor, tall pillars and crystal statues in alcoves. V.O.: *"Yes, a* magic *circle."*
3. **The summoners (0:35–0:55).** Boot scuffs echo in the dark. Five robed figures step into the circle's light and surround him, muttering and staring. V.O.: *"What was I, a fucking zoo animal?"* One throws his arms around another: *"We did it! The summoning… the summoning was a success!"* He gets shoved off. Close on Erdan: *"Summoning?"*, and he realizes he understands a language he's never heard.
4. **Sunlight (0:55–1:20).** A hooded woman suddenly grabs both his hands; he flinches. *"O brave hero! Please, save our world from destruction!"* The circle dims. A robed man presses a stone in the wall, a heavy curtain slides open, and sunlight floods in through drifting dust, making the crystal statues shimmer. Reveal Kina's freckles and blonde hair. The old man lowers his hood (Mernel): *"Great hero… would you lend us your strength for the sake of the kingdom — no, the* world*?"* Erdan: *"Huh?"* Hold on his face. V.O., quieter: *"I didn't want to* do *anything… I just wanted to hide under a blanket. Back to my games. Back to my family."*
5. **"Unique appearance" (1:20–1:45).** In the full light Mernel blinks in surprise, and Kina lets go of Erdan's hands. Erdan looks down at his own dark-toned hands. V.O.: *"…Oh. Are they surprised that I'm black?"* Mernel, with a forced smile and a glance at Erdan's hair: *"I was simply surprised by your… unique appearance…"* V.O.: *"Wow, nicely put, old man."* Mernel waves his hands, *"I didn't mean it that way!"*, and Kina smacks the back of his head: *"Mernel, watch your words! You're speaking to the hero!"* Both bow deeply. Erdan, awkwardly raising a hand: *"I-it's fine, please raise your heads. I'm not offended…"*
6. **The name (1:45–2:00).** Walking down a sunlit corridor, flanked by the robed group. The nervous man: *"Hero, might I ask for your name?"* Erdan: *"Uhh… my name? It's A…"* He stops and mumbles. Another summoner hisses at the man for asking so casually. Erdan, with a small private grin, delivering the alias he prepared long ago: *"Erdan… Erdan Kernt. That's my name."*
7. **The veiled Queen (2:00–2:30).** The throne room. Erdan kneels clumsily, copying Mernel and Kina. He peeks up at the red veil hiding the throne. V.O.: *"Was the king too ugly to show himself?"* A woman's voice from behind it: *"Welcome to the Kingdom of Elysandria, 32nd Hero of Aleria. I am the Queen… Elisabeth Bernt Ravenshield III."* V.O.: *"It's a* Queen*?"* She explains the Lumenari (*"Last time, ignoring one wiped out four nations in a single day"*), and Erdan's eyes widen. V.O.: *"Fuck nah!"* Then she explains the Doomsday, a flood of monsters from the underworld. Erdan mumbles, *"Do I get some kind of ability?"*, and the room goes silent. *"Could you speak up louder?"* Mernel taps his shoulder and relays the question for him. V.O.: *"Thank you for the save, Mernel!"*
8. **The guidebook (2:30–2:55).** A servant steps out from behind the veil with a thick red book with gold letters: *A Guide for Heroes*, by the 24th hero, Light Kagami. Erdan opens it and finds it's all in Japanese. He turns to Mernel, puzzled; Mernel is just as puzzled. The servant swaps it for the *"Ingleesh"* edition. Close-up of the first page: *"Great hero! … Be grateful, peasant! PS: There is no video-game-like interface, so give up on that idea."* Erdan flips on: every page is blank, and the book grows thicker the more he flips. He snaps it shut and it shrinks back. Queen: *"Well? Do you understand the context now, hero?"* Erdan: *"Y-yeah… thanks for the book. Your Majesty."*
9. **Home? (2:55–3:05).** Erdan, louder this time: *"Will I be able to return home after I finish my mission?"* A pause. Queen: *"…Yes, it is possible."* He lowers his eyes. V.O.: *"What a pain in the ass."* Then, softer, over a brief image of a crowded family dinner table: *"I just wish I could've told them I wasn't dead in a ditch somewhere."*
10. **The Core Seal (3:05–3:15).** The black-stone testing chamber, its blue sigils pulsing. Kina, teasing: *"It'll sting a little. But you're a strong boy, aren't you?"* Erdan: *"Yeah… thanks for the warning."* He presses his hand onto the imprint. One beat of silence, then light and glowing symbols slam up his arm into his head, and the frame whites out. Title card: **A GUIDE FOR HEROES (WHO'D RATHER NOT BE)**.

**Sound:** muffled anime audio in the bedroom, then dead silence in the dark. A soft heartbeat-like thrum for the chest glow and a low hum from the magic circle. Boot scuffs and robe rustle, all with big stone-hall reverb. The heavy curtain sliding and a warm swell of sunlight. A light, comedic smack for Kina's slap. Footsteps echo in the corridor; the throne room is quiet with a murmuring court. The book's pages flutter, then thud shut. The Core Seal's runes pulse like a heartbeat, ending in a rising magical surge. Keep the music sparse: curious woodwinds and soft strings, turning warm and wistful on "Back to my family." No cartoonish, squeaky or wet body sounds; movement should sound natural.

**Negative prompt:** 2D, flat anime shading, cartoon, fast cuts, rushed dialogue, text artifacts other than the book page, morphing faces, extra limbs, changing skin tone or hair, jittery camera, sudden style changes, showing the Queen's face clearly.
